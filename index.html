Process

How we work.

A small client list means every search gets proper attention.

  1. Brief and market mapping

    We spend time understanding your current stage, culture and exactly what good looks like before we start searching.

    The brief is where most agencies cut corners. We don't.

    01

  2. Active search, not a database

    Every search is curated from scratch. We go to market directly, find people suited to your brief and approach them thoughtfully on your behalf.

    We build every list fresh. Your search is never recycled.

    02

  3. Honest shortlists

    We'd rather send you three people we genuinely believe in than ten to hit a number. Every candidate comes with a clear view of fit.

    Three names you'll want to meet. Not ten to fill a deck.

    03

  4. End-to-end support

    We stay closely connected through interview prep, offer stage and notice periods. The role isn't complete when the shortlist lands.

    Still here at offer. Still here at start date.

    04

Prefer to do things differently?We're happy to shape our process around the way your team already likes to work.

I started Boundly because placing GTM talent and understanding GTM teams are two different things. Most agencies know how to fill a role. Far fewer understand how that role sits within the wider business, and care about how this placement will shape the company in the future.

That combination of knowing how agencies work and knowing what it actually feels like to be responsible for the hire is what Boundly is built on. I work with a small number of clients at a time, so every search gets my full attention from start to finish.

If you're building a GTM team at any stage, from pre-seed to enterprise, and want to work with someone who's been on both sides of the table, I'd love to talk.
