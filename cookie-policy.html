1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — such as your preferences or how you navigated the site — and can improve your experience on future visits.

Some cookies are essential for the site to function. Others are used for analytics or personalisation and require your consent.

2. Cookies we use
